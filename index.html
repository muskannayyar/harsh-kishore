How I think.

Three rules I always follow. This is how my thinking works.

First principles

Start from what’s true, not from how it’s always been done.

My view+

Strip the problem back to what’s actually true, then rebuild from there. It stops me solving the wrong problem beautifully.

See the whole system

Process, data, architecture and people move together. Treat them as one.

My view+

Each one affects the others. Fix one in isolation and you just move the problem, so I solve for all four at once.

Relentless value

If it doesn’t move cash, speed or service, it doesn’t ship.

My view+

Everything has to earn its place. I’d rather ship less that matters than more that doesn’t.
